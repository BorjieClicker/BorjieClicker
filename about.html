👤 Who is Borjie?

Hey! I'm Borjie — the person behind this whole ridiculous clicker game. I'm just someone who spends way too much time on the internet, clicks things for fun, and at some point decided: "what if I made other people click things too?"

By day I'm a normal human being. By night I'm engineering the most absurd idle game mechanics I can think of, balancing crit multipliers at 2am, and wondering if anyone will ever reach Cosmic Borjie rank.

I go by Borjie online. Yes, it's a real nickname. No, I won't explain it further.

🛠️ Why I Built This

Honestly? I wanted to see if I could. I've always loved incremental games — the satisfying loop of watching numbers get bigger, unlocking new things, and the dopamine hit of a golden Borjie appearing at just the right moment.

So I sat down and built Borjie Clicker from scratch — pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no frameworks, no engines. Just vibes and an unhealthy number of hours. It started small and kept growing: rebirths, combo mechanics, frenzy events, special upgrades, emoji currency tiers…

If you're reading this, you've somehow found the About page, which means you're either genuinely curious or you've been clicking for so long you needed a break. Either way — thank you for playing. 💜
